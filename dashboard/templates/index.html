Understanding the changes

Negative values indicate a decrease in radar backscatter between the two observations. Positive values indicate an increase.

HH Polarization

HH backscatter can respond to changes in surface roughness, moisture conditions and vegetation structure

HV Polarization

HV backscatter can provide information about vegetation structure and volume scattering.

These differences represent radar-observed changes and are not direct measurements of soil moisture or crop health.
