EXPLANATION

What is Antheia investigating?

Antheia explores spatial and temporal variations in NASA-ISRO NISAR L-band HH and HV radar backscatter across Faisalabad District.

Project Conclusion

CURRENT STUDY

Antheia demonstrates how satellite radar observations can be processed and compared to identify changes in the radar response across Faisalabad District.

HH MEDIAN CHANGE −0.212 dB
HV MEDIAN CHANGE −0.170 dB

Both displayed district median changes are negative, meaning the later observation has a lower median backscatter value than the earlier observation for these comparisons.

What this conclusion does not claim

These radar differences do not, by themselves, prove that soil moisture or crop health decreased, or explain why the signal changed. Additional datasets and field observations would be needed to test those causes.

Scientific Investigation Board

Three investigations that connect the processing workflow to the questions the data can answer.

ANALYSIS
01

Temporal Backscatter Analysis

Did the radar response change between the two acquisitions?

Approach

Compare HH and HV observations from 30 October and 11 November 2026, then examine their difference maps and district statistics.

Finding

The dashboard reports median changes of −0.212 dB for HH and −0.170 dB for HV.

The change describes the radar signal, not a proven change in a specific ground condition.
02

Polarization Comparison

Do HH and HV provide different views of the surface?

Approach

Examine co-polarized HH and cross-polarized HV maps and compare their temporal responses.

Finding

The two polarizations provide complementary radar observations. Their responses can be influenced by different scattering mechanisms.

A difference between HH and HV is not, on its own, a crop classification or vegetation-health result.
03

Spatial Data Reliability

How does Antheia make the comparison more consistent?

Approach

Apply quality masking, extract the Faisalabad boundary, and compare temporal observations using common valid pixels.

Finding

The workflow restricts analysis to the study area and uses pixels valid in both observations for temporal comparison.

Masking improves comparability but does not remove every source of measurement uncertainty.
