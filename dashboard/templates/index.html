SCIENTIFIC ANALYSIS

Understanding HH Backscatter

HH backscatter represents the horizontally transmitted and horizontally received radar signal.

Scientific consideration

Radar backscatter can respond to surface roughness, moisture conditions and vegetation structure. A backscatter difference alone cannot establish a specific physical cause.
